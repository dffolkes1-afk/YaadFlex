❓ YaadFlex Global Vybz – Insurance FAQ

Q: Are YaadFlex drivers insured?
A: Yes. All our red plate taxi and PPV drivers carry their own motor vehicle insurance, as required by Jamaican law. This covers accidents, damages, and third-party liability while they are on the road.


---

Q: Does YaadFlex itself have insurance?
A: Yes. YaadFlex carries business-level protection, including:

✅ General Liability – covers accidents, injuries, or damages involving the company.

✅ Professional Liability (Errors & Omissions) – covers system errors, booking issues, or technology mistakes.

✅ Cyber Liability – covers data breaches and keeps your information safe.



---

Q: Do I need travel insurance when booking trips or excursions?
A: Travel insurance is optional but recommended. YaadFlex offers customer travel insurance as an add-on when booking tours or trips. It covers medical emergencies, luggage, cancellations, and delays. The cost depends on trip length and destination, but averages about J$36,000 per 8-day trip.


---

Q: Who pays for vehicle insurance?
A: Drivers are responsible for their own PPV (red plate) vehicle insurance. This is not handled by YaadFlex.


---

Q: Does YaadFlex insure my data?
A: Yes. Our cyber insurance ensures that if there’s a data breach or attack, your personal and payment information is covered and protected.


---

Q: Why does this matter?
A: Insurance is about trust and safety. By protecting our riders, drivers, and digital systems, YaadFlex ensures peace of mind every time you use the app.
