Here’s the recap of what we worked in and out so far for YardFlex:


---

🌍 YardFlex – Full Blueprint (so far)

🔹 Core Vision

A Caribbean Uber-style super app for:

🚕 Rides (passenger transport)

📦 Deliveries (from bike to truck)

🛒 E-commerce + instant delivery (groceries, hardware, furniture, etc.)




---

🔹 Drivers & Riders

Radar Riders → all delivery riders (bicycles, e-bikes, motorbikes).

Radar Cars → small cars, private white plates that do deliveries.

Radar Vans & Trucks → for big packages, hardware runs, or moving house.

Everyone with wheels can join YardFlex and earn.



---

🔹 Pricing Logic

Distance → base + per km/mile.

Package size/weight → small / medium / bulk (pressure weight system).

Urgency (time):

Instant (🚀 fastest, most expensive)

Same Day (⏳ medium)

Scheduled/Next Day (📅 cheapest)


Pooling → packages in the same direction roll together → driver earns more, customer pays less.



---

🔹 Payments

Customers can pay:

💳 Card (Visa/MasterCard)

💵 Cash on pickup/delivery

📲 YardFlex Wallet (like Link)


Drivers get paid out:

Daily / Weekly / Monthly (they choose).




---

🔹 Features

Radar Tracking → every rider/driver visible on the app.

Instant Delivery Mode → costs more but driver comes right away.

Flex Delivery Mode → wait a bit, costs less.

E-commerce Layer → customers buy goods + YardFlex handles delivery.

Multi-Vehicle Support → from a bicycle up to a moving truck.

Weight Calculator → app factors package “pressure weight” into pricing so both driver and customer know what’s being carried.



---

🔹 Business Side

Register YardFlex Limited.

Integrate payment gateway (Stripe, WiPay, or FAC).

Draft Terms & Privacy Policy (for app stores + payments).



---

🔹 Tech Side

Frontend: React Native or Flutter (customer + driver apps).

Backend: Node.js + Firebase/Supabase (bookings, payments, tracking).

Database: Stores users, vehicles, payments.

Google Maps API: Radar + routing.
