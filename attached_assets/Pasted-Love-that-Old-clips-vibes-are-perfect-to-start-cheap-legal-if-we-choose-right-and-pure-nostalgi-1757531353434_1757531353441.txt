What to show (safe sources)

1. Public-domain & CC videos (free to use)



Internet Archive has tons of films and vintage clips you can embed/host from them. Start here: Moving Image Archive. 

Example titles often marked public domain:

“Jamaica Inn” (1939, Hitchcock) – public domain copies on IA. (Not Jamaican, but on-brand for the name.) 

“The Treasure of Jamaica Reef” (1975) – listed as Public Domain on IA. 

Caribbean culture docs/footage (Carnival etc.) with permissive rights on IA. 



2. Historic newsreels (embed from official channels)



British Pathé has loads of Jamaica newsreels (1950s–60s). You can embed their YouTube uploads (allowed under YouTube terms) or license from Pathé later. 


> ⚠️ Avoid famous Jamaican features like The Harder They Come or Rockers—they’re not public domain.
