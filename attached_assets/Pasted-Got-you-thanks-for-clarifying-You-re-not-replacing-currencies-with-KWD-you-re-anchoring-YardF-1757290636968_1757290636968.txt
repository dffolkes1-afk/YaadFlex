🔗 YardFlex Coin Model (Illustration for Replit)

1. Core Concept

YardFlex Coin (YFX) is the internal currency.

Pegged to KWD: 1 YFX always equals 1 Kuwaiti Dinar.

Used for pricing, driver payouts, and cross-border balance.

Externally, users see their local currency (JMD, USD, etc.).



---

2. Example Flow

Rider books a trip in Kingston.

Fare = $1,200 JMD.

Backend converts $1,200 JMD → 2.4 YFX (since ~1 KWD ≈ 500 JMD).

Driver’s payout = 2.04 YFX (85%).

YardFlex keeps 0.36 YFX (15%).


In the app:

Rider sees JMD amount.

Driver sees balance in JMD or switches to YFX view.

YardFlex core ledger always runs in YFX (pegged to KWD).




---

3. Why This Matters

Stable foundation → avoids JMD/USD fluctuation headaches.

Global scalability → payments in Africa, Asia, America all normalized to YFX.

Crypto-ready → if YardFlex Coin becomes tokenized later, it’s already pegged to the world’s strongest fiat currency (KWD).

5. Technical Implementation (for the devs)

Ledger Base: All balances stored in YFX.

Conversion Layer: Real-time FX rates (JMD↔YFX, USD↔YFX, etc.) via API.

User Setting: Default display currency = local, with option to view in YFX.

Smart Contract (optional future): YFX minted/burned to keep 1:1 with KWD.
