🚖 YaadFlex Driver & Pricing Policy Blueprint

1. Fleet Structure

Red Plate Fleet (“Lipstick Fleet”)

Core official taxis.

Operate openly in municipal/city areas.

Main public face of YaadFlex.


White Plate Fleet (“Shadow Fleet”)

Selective, limited drivers.

Operate quietly in rural and uptown areas where red plates are scarce.

Focus more on errands, deliveries, and small trips.

Not openly promoted in municipal zones (to avoid police conflict).




---

2. Automatic Job Assignment

Jobs are assigned by the YaadFlex system (not chosen by drivers).

Prevents cherry-picking and ensures fair distribution.

Algorithm balances based on:

Location & distance

Driver availability

Workload balance (so everybody eats)

Vehicle type (taxi, errand car, bike, e-rider, delivery van, etc.)




---

3. Multiple Errand Grouping

If errands are in the same area & timeframe, the system groups them.

Assigned to one driver automatically → saves fuel & time.

Drivers cannot reject smaller errands to wait on bigger ones.



---

4. Area-Based Pricing

Downtown → Lower base fares, accessible for workers.

Uptown / Premium Zones → Slightly higher fares, reflecting demand & lifestyle.

Rural Areas → Balanced fares with fuel compensation for drivers.



---

5. Lifestyle & Add-On Charges

Waiting Time: First 5 minutes free, then small per-minute fee.

Smoke / Chill Stops: Extra fee added for each stop.

Extra Stop / Pick Up Friend: Fixed charge per stop.

Eating in Car: Cleaning surcharge.

Extra Passenger / Overload: Fee per additional passenger beyond comfort/legal limit.

Party Runs: Slight surcharge for late-night party/club pickups.

Tourist & Excursion Trips: +15% surcharge.

Hilly/Uphill Areas: +10% surcharge.

Airport Trips: Travelers pay extra; airport workers = normal fare.



---

6. Balance Rule – “Fair But Fun”

Riders can still enjoy themselves (stop, lime, smoke, eat, party), but all extras are fairly priced.

Charges are light enough not to crush customers, but strong enough to protect driver earnings.

Goal: Passengers feel freedom, drivers feel rewarded, YaadFlex grows with balance.



---

✅ Summary:

Red Plates rule the city.

White Plates fill rural gaps discreetly.

App assigns jobs automatically (drivers don’t pick).

Area + Lifestyle pricing keeps fares fair but profitable.

Everybody eats, nobody feels crushed.
