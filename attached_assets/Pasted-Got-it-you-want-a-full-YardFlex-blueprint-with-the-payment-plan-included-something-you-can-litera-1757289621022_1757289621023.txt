🚀 YardFlex – Full Blueprint

2. Core Services

Taxi Rides – regular, airport, hills (10% surcharge), full car loads, peak-hour pricing.

Car Rentals – short-term & long-term.

School Carpooling – trusted family & kids transport.

Food Delivery – restaurants, shops, supermarkets.

Tours & Excursions – 15% surcharge for tour operators & excursion zones.

Insurance Integration – direct partner integration.

Job Listings – hustlers & riders find quick jobs.

Non-App Bookings – SMS, hotline, kiosks, cash/vouchers supported.

3. Payment System

Accepted methods:

Debit/Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, local NCB/Scotia links).

Mobile wallets (NCB Quisk, Lynk, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay).

Cash rides & vouchers for non-app users.


Driver payout options: daily, weekly, fortnightly, or monthly.

Auto split system:

Rider pays into central YardFlex account.

Automated split:

Driver → % payout (covers fuel + profit).

YardFlex → % platform fee.

Govt./Tour surcharge (if applicable).

Insurance (if applied).


Everything routed online — no manual handoff.




---

4. Pricing Blueprint

Base fare: Standard local market competitive rate.

Add-ons:

Airport passenger trips → extra fee.

Airport workers → no extra fee.

Tours/excursions → 15% surcharge.

Hills → +10%.

Waiting stop → first 5 mins free, after that charged.

Peak hour & traffic multipliers.


Choice for passenger:

Fixed fare (great for waiting/stopping).

Time-based fare (better for direct trips).
