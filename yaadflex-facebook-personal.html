My Story & Why YaadFlex 📖

Growing up in Jamaica, I watched talented people struggle to make ends meet while big corporations took most of the profits from their hard work. That never sat right with me.

When I saw Uber and Lyft paying drivers only 70-75% of fares, I knew Jamaica could do better. We've always been pioneers - in music, sports, culture - so why not in fair technology?

YaadFlex isn't just an app - it's a movement. It's proof that small islands can create big changes. It's our way of showing the world that technology should serve people, not exploit them.

Every feature in YaadFlex comes from listening to real Jamaicans: the taxi driver who needs fair pay, the grandmother who wants simple money transfers, the local business owner who deserves support.

This is bigger than business - it's about taking Jamaica to the Moon and bringing the world with us! 🚀
