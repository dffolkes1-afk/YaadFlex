🚖 YaadFlex Credits (Digital Top-Up System)

1. Top-Up Flow (Rider Side)

Step 1: Rider opens app → taps “Top-Up Balance” on home screen.

Step 2: Chooses amount (JA$500, JA$1,000, JA$2,000, or custom).

Step 3: Selects payment method:

Link wallet

Debit/Credit card (Visa/Mastercard)

Bank transfer (NCB, Scotia, JN, etc.)


Step 4: Confirms → balance updates instantly → “You now have JA$1,500 YaadFlex Credits.”



---

2. Ride/Delivery Payment Flow

Rider books ride → fare is auto-deducted from credits balance.

Confirmation screen: “This ride cost JA$750. Remaining balance: JA$750.”

If balance too low → prompt: “Top up now to complete booking.”



---

3. Driver Payout Flow

Driver finishes trips → earnings show in driver dashboard as pending balance.

Cash-Out Options:

Direct to Link wallet (instant)

Bank account transfer (1–2 business days)


Driver chooses → payout processed → confirmation sent.



---

4. Admin/Business Dashboard (You)

See total credits purchased daily/weekly/monthly.

Monitor rides paid with credits vs cash.

Export reports for bank settlement.

Optional: set bonus top-ups (e.g. “Top up $2,000, get +200 credits free”).



---

5. Future Add-Ons

Digicel/Flow airtime top-up as a funding method.

Peer-to-peer transfer (send credits between users).

Promo credits for loyal riders/drivers.

Integration with food delivery and errands so one balance pays for everything.



---

✅ Why This Flow Works

Keeps it simple: Credits = rides. No complex wallet regulations yet.

Gives flexibility: Riders can choose how to fund it, drivers can choose how to cash out.

Scales naturally: When ready, you can expand this into a BOJ-regulated full wallet.
