Campaign Blueprint Tips

Use bold visuals, bright colors, and playful language.
Feature real teens, genuine stories, and user-generated content.
Highlight rewards and instant benefits in every post.
Leverage TikTok, Instagram Reels, and short videos.
Run weekly challenges and update leaderboards publicly.
Spotlight ambassadors and top users every month.


This blueprint is designed to make teens feel seen, empowered, and rewarded—turning every ride into a chance to flex, win, and belong. Copy, customize, and launch your campaign across all social channels for maximum hype!
