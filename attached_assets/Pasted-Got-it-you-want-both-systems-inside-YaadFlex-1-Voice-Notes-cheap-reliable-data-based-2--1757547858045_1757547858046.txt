🔐 Safe Dual Communication System for YaadFlex

1. Voice Notes (Default Mode)

Riders & drivers use in-app voice notes for 90% of communication.

Cost to you = almost nothing (server bandwidth only).

Works even when internet is weak → better reliability than calls in some rural areas.

All messages are encrypted end-to-end.


2. Emergency/Masked Calls (Fallback Mode)

If rider/driver taps “Call” (especially in emergencies), system dials through VoIP.

Provider (e.g., CommPeak, Twilio, Newik-VoIP) bridges the internet call → PSTN.

Both parties see a YaadFlex number, not their real number (safety).

You control duration + cost limits (e.g., 2–3 minutes max).



---

💳 Payment Structure (Delaware Model)

You register your business entity in Delaware (U.S.).

All payments to communication providers (Twilio, CommPeak, etc.) are billed through your Delaware company.

This gives you:

U.S. credibility for global providers.

Access to better VoIP rates.

One payment stream instead of messy local/regional accounts.




---

⚡ How It Plays Out in Real Life

Normal day: Riders & drivers exchange quick voice notes (“I’m outside,” “Coming in 5 mins”). → No cost to you.

Emergency case: Rider’s data drops, or they need immediate contact → They tap “Call,” system routes via VoIP → Driver’s phone rings like a normal call. → Small per-minute cost to you, but worth it for safety.



---

✅ Why This is the Safest Setup

Privacy: No rider/driver ever sees personal numbers.

Control: You decide when a call is allowed and how long it runs.

Cost-Efficiency: 95% of comms = free voice notes, only rare emergency calls = paid.

Scalable: Delaware base lets you expand across the Caribbean without renegotiating with each island telco.
